> - dev and prod: Kubernetes manifests including Envoy Gateway routes, and Pulumi inputs.
>
> Nothing per environment is written by hand, and a test fails if an overlay changes something that belongs in the model. Today's hand-written local files are the starting point and are replaced by the renderer in Phase 2.
