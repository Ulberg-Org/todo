❓ **Q16** - **Pin Kubernetes 1.37 for local and cloud, in one place.** The *minor* version is identical everywhere. ❓ **Q18** - **One "platform CLI" for everything local.** Setting up the local cluster belongs to the platform: kind, Cilium, Envoy Gateway, ArgoCD and the telemetry charts. Rendering is the platform's job too. Proposal: the platform repo ships **one public container image**, `platform`, that does both:
- `platform up`: create the cluster, install the platform components, render `platform.yaml` for local, and deploy the app through ArgoCD;
- `platform down`, `platform test` (the network rules).

The todo repo's `make up` just runs that image with the pinned version. App developers never touch platform internals, and a platform change reaches every app by bumping one version.

➡️ Yes: built, scanned and signed by platform CI like our app image, and public on GitHub's registry, since the repos are public anyway.
