The full version works end to end. In the confined Lima VM, with everything going through `srt`'s allowlisting proxy, `make sync` brings all 13 Argo CD applications to synced and healthy, and all 12 network checks pass. Claude Code runs in there with your login.

The last problem was our own network policy: Argo CD may reach the internet only on port 443, and the proxy listens on another port. The CLI now creates a small extra policy for exactly the proxy's address and port when there is a proxy.
