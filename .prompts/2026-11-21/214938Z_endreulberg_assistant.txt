I found the cause, read from the pod's log as Claude's agent, and the fix is up as **platform #65**.

**What went wrong:** Azure's Entra functions (`pgaadauth_create_principal` and friends) only exist in the server's **`postgres`** database, and the Job ran everything in the `todo` database. The shared `roles.sql` had gone through: schema, roles and privileges were fine.

**The fix:**
- **Two steps:** `roles.sql` runs in the app's database, then `azure.sql` in `postgres`, where Azure's functions are. Roles are server-wide anyway.
- **One transaction each:** a failure leaves nothing half-done, so a retry starts clean. Before, the first pod had already committed `roles.sql`, so its retries would have failed with "role already exists" instead of showing the real error.
- **The misplaced workflow comment** is fixed in the same PR.

Checked in the sandbox: style, build, Environment tests 12/12.
