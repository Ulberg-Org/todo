The research changes Q1. Here's what people actually use Lima for, and what that means for us.

## What I found

**1. Nobody has a mature Lima setup that blocks the internet.**
- Lima's own egress-policy work (issue #5820, PR #4326) is open, unmerged, and a maintainer is "inclined to close it".
- Trail of Bits' `coop` (822 stars) runs agents in Lima but leaves egress open on macOS. Its proxy only keeps API keys out of the VM.
- In Lima a guest with root can get around any firewall inside the VM, because Lima's host process recreates every guest connection as an ordinary connection from your Mac.

**2. The only robust design is to restrict that host process, not the VM.**
- Two small projects do it (apomonosi/sandboxing PR #20 and boxery PR #103, both merged in autumn 2026). They start `limactl` under macOS's `sandbox-exec`, which Lima's host process inherits, so it may only connect to the proxy port and the VM's SSH port on your Mac.
- The VM's only way out is then the proxy, which allows the allowlist and nothing else. Your Mac's kernel enforces this, so root in the VM can't lift it.
- Caveats: Apple has deprecated `sandbox-exec`, though it still ships and works on macOS 26. Nobody has reported it on 27.2, which you run. apomonosi says it's "not yet a live install". Neither project meets our supply-chain rule, so I'd copy the pattern, not the code.

**3. Anthropic ships that exact pattern as an official tool, [`sandbox-runtime`](https://github.com/anthropics/sandbox-runtime) (`srt`).**
- On macOS it starts a program under `sandbox-exec` and allows network only through its own allowlisting proxy.
- Its [secure-deployment guide](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/secure-deployment) recommends that design: "a connection to an outside host that skips the proxy fails".
- So we could start the VM through `srt` (`srt limactl start …`) and get the proxy plus the confinement from an official source, without writing our own.

**4. kind with Cilium in Lima is plausible but not proven recently.**
- Lima, `vz` and Cilium with kind worked in 2023 ([bcollard](https://github.com/bcollard/kind-on-lima-public)), and kubeadm with Cilium in Lima worked in January 2026 ([ypeavler](https://ypeavler.github.io/blog/local/kubernetes/2026/01/17/k8s-cluster-with-lima-macos.html)).
- Neither is a recent report of exactly our setup.

## Proposal: a short spike before building

The open points are all facts, so I'd settle them with a time-limited test rather than guess:
1. **Our platform in Lima:** a Lima `vz` Ubuntu VM with Docker and no mounts, then `make up` and `make test-platform` inside it.
2. **The egress block:** start that VM through `srt` on your Mac 27.2 and check from inside as root that the allowlisted hosts work while a direct connection to anything else, your Mac's other ports and your LAN all fail.

If both hold, I build on that. If `srt` or `sandbox-exec` can't confine Lima on 27.2, I'll come back to you with the options rather than guess.
