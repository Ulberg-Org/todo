**What the Docker Sandboxes docs settle:**
- **Docker inside:** each sandbox has its own Docker engine, with no path to your Mac's, so `make up` with kind runs inside it.
- **Credentials:** tokens stay in your Mac's keychain, and a proxy on your Mac adds them to outgoing requests. The sandbox only ever sees a placeholder, which fits D78.
- **Network:** a "deny-all" mode where every destination needs an explicit allow rule.
- **Against D78:** SSH agent forwarding is **on** by default, which would let the sandbox use your SSH keys. Our setup will turn it off.
- **Not personal-free:** `sbx` needs a sign-in with a Docker account (`sbx login`). That's the developer's account, used only by the tool on your Mac; nothing of it enters the sandbox.
